## Privacy Policy

### What Actually Happens

Your typed amount and chosen currency never leave your device. This is not merely a promise — it's a commitment written into the code. 🐾

When you convert, your device sends one request to a public currency rate server — `open.er-api.com`, or `api.frankfurter.dev` if the first is down. One of these servers replies with today's currency rates. Done.

FX Convert does not:
- Save or remember your numbers
- Use cookies or analytics

It doesn't track you. It doesn't store you. It doesn't know you. Then again, it's a cat — it was never really paying attention to you anyway.

Everything you type lives only in your browser's memory. Close the tab and it's gone, just like a cat the moment you want it on your lap.

### One Honest ~~Pawnote~~ Footnote

Only four parties are involved in serving this website:

| Who | What they see |
|-----|---------------|
| **GitHub** (hosts this page) | Your IP address, logged for security — as any web host does |
| **Cloudflare** (routes the currency rate requests) | Your IP address as it delivers the request |

| **Your device** | Everything you type. The maths happens right there, locally — for your eyes only |
