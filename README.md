## Privacy Policy

### What Actually Happens

Your typed amount and chosen currency never leave your device. This is not merely a promise — it's a commitment written into the code. 🐾

The app downloads current rates when the page loads, and again whenever you tap 'Update Rates'. Each download is one request to a public currency rate server — open.er-api.com, or api.frankfurter.dev (if the primary one is down). One of these servers replies with today's currency rates. Done.

FX Convert does not:
- Save or remember your numbers
- Use cookies or analytics

It doesn't track you. It doesn't store you. It doesn't know you. Then again, it's a cat — it was never really paying attention to you anyway.

Everything you type lives only in your browser's memory. Close the tab and it's gone, just like a cat the moment you want it on your lap.

### An Honest ~~Pawnote~~ Footnote

Only three parties are involved in serving this website:

| Who | What they see |
|-----|---------------|
| **GitHub** (hosts this page) | Your IP address, logged for security — as any web host does |
| **The currency rate servers** (`open.er-api.com`, `api.frankfurter.dev`) | Your IP address, as with any web request — and the same fixed currency rate request every visitor sends; nothing personal in it |
| **Your device** | Everything you type. The maths happens right there, locally — for your eyes only |

## Credits & Catnip

This cat doesn't hunt its own rates. Fresh figures are demanded from two public servers, summoned the moment you ask:

- **[Exchange Rate API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com)** — the primary bowl. Daily rates for a bowlful of currencies, served free and without an API key. Their one ask is a credit line, which you'll also find in the app's footer where the rates actually appear.
- **[Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev)** — the backup bowl, served when the first is empty. Open source, and stocked with reference rates blended from dozens of central banks and monetary authorities.

Both bowls run on kindness and zero API keys.
