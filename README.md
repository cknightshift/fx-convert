## Privacy Policy

### What Actually Happens

Your typed amount and chosen currency never leave your device. This is not merely a promise — it's a commitment scratched into FX Convert's code. 🐾

The web app downloads current rates when the page loads, and again whenever you tap 'Update Rates'.

FX Convert does not:
- Save or remember your numbers
- Use cookies or analytics

FX Convert itself doesn't track you. It doesn't store you. It doesn't know you. Then again, it's a cat — it was never really paying attention to you anyway.

Everything you type lives only in your browser's memory. Close the tab and it's gone, just like a cat the moment you want it on your lap.

### An Honest ~~Pawnote~~ Footnote

These parties are involved in serving this web app:

- **GitHub web host** — as with any website, your device's IP address is received as part of serving the page.
- **Currency rate servers** (`open.er-api.com`, `api.frankfurter.dev`) — the web app sends the same fixed currency rate request every visitor sends, so nothing you typed travels with it. As with any website, these servers receive your device's IP address as part of serving the request.
- **You and your device** — everything you type. The arithmetic happens right there, locally on your device, for your eyes only.
